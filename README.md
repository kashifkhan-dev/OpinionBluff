# Impostor
### Defend the Undefendable

Impostor is a thrilling party game of deception, social deduction, and persuasion. One player is the **Impostor**, while the others form the **Honest Group**. The goal is simple: don't get caught, or find the liar before time runs out.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **The Reveal**: Pass the phone around. Each player reveals their secret topic. The Honest Group sees the real topic, while the Impostor sees a fake one.
2. **The Discussion**: Start a heated debate! Defend your opinion strictly. The Impostor must blend in and convince others they know the real topic.
3. **The Vote**: Once the timer ends, everyone votes on who they think is the Impostor.
4. **The Result**: If the majority catches the Impostor, the group wins. If the Impostor escapes, they win, and the group faces the punishment!

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-language Support**: Play in English, French, or Spanish.
- **Categorized Punishments**: Variety of challenges from Low to Very Hard.
- **Customizable Experience**: Choose between Same Topic or Mixed Topic modes.
- **Premium Design**: Modern, glassmorphism-inspired UI with smooth animations.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Flutter](https://flutter.dev)
- **State Management**: Provider
- **UI Library**: [cupertino_native_better](https://pub.dev/packages/cupertino_native_better)
- **Localizations**: Custom multi-language system
